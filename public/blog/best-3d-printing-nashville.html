Best 3D Printing Services in Nashville (2026)

6 min read Nashville, TN

Nashville has transformed from a music industry hub into one of the South's fastest-growing technology and healthcare cities. HCA Healthcare, Vanderbilt University Medical Center, and a dense cluster of health IT companies make Nashville a significant healthcare market, while a booming construction and hospitality sector drives demand for architectural models, signage prototyping, and custom fabrication. The 3D printing market here is growing fast to match.

This guide covers Nashville's 3D printing landscape, where services are located, and what to expect for different project types.

The Nashville 3D Printing Landscape

3D printing services in the Nashville metro cluster around the city's primary industry zones:

  • Midtown / West End — Vanderbilt University, medical prototyping, design studios
  • The Gulch / Downtown — architecture firms, hospitality design, startup prototyping
  • Cool Springs / Brentwood — healthcare IT companies, suburban industrial shops
  • Antioch / Murfreesboro Road Corridor — manufacturing and automotive suppliers
  • Smyrna / Murfreesboro — Nissan automotive supply chain, industrial fabrication

FDM Printing in Nashville

FDM is widely available in Nashville and expanding quickly. Downtown and Midtown shops serve architecture, interior design, and startup clients with presentation models and functional prototypes. Suburban shops in the Cool Springs and Smyrna corridors serve the automotive supply chain — Nissan's assembly plant in Smyrna creates sustained demand for jigs, fixtures, and tooling printed in engineering-grade materials including PETG, ASA, and Nylon.

Nashville's booming hospitality and entertainment industry also creates unusual demand for large-format FDM for props, themed fabrications, and bespoke interior design elements — a niche that several local shops have begun to specialize in.

SLA / Resin Printing in Nashville

SLA services in Nashville are driven primarily by the healthcare and dental markets. Vanderbilt Medical Center and Nashville's large DSO and dental lab community create consistent demand for surgical guides, dental models, and device housings. Several shops near the West End medical corridor stock dental and biocompatible resins and offer same-day turnaround for dental labs with urgent requirements.

Healthcare Hub

Nashville is the headquarters of more hospital companies per capita than any other US city, including HCA Healthcare. The resulting healthcare ecosystem drives strong demand for medical-grade resin printing — shops here are well-versed in biocompatible materials and device prototyping workflows.

SLS and Industrial Printing in Nashville

SLS and MJF services in the Nashville area are primarily available from shops in the suburban industrial corridors — Brentwood, Antioch, and the Murfreesboro area. Automotive supply chain clients drive demand for durable nylon parts, and several shops offer production-capable equipment for batch runs. As Nashville's manufacturing base has grown, so has demand for functional end-use parts in engineering-grade nylon rather than just one-off prototypes.

Metal 3D Printing in Nashville

Metal additive manufacturing is less concentrated in Nashville than in larger industrial metros, but several shops in the Smyrna and Murfreesboro corridors offer DMLS services primarily for automotive and aerospace suppliers. For larger or more specialized metal printing jobs, some Nashville clients work with service bureaus in Atlanta or Charlotte — both within overnight shipping range.

Healthcare / dental?

Midtown and West End shops near Vanderbilt specialize in dental and medical device resin printing with fast turnaround

Automotive / industrial?

Smyrna and Murfreesboro corridor shops serve Nashville's Nissan supply chain with engineering-grade FDM and SLS

University and Makerspace Access

Vanderbilt University's design school and engineering departments have well-equipped fabrication labs available to enrolled students. Tennessee State University and Belmont University also have maker resources on campus. Make Nashville is a community makerspace with 3D printing access for members, and the Nashville Public Library system offers basic FDM printing at the main branch.

Tips for Ordering 3D Prints in Nashville

  • Growing market: Nashville's 3D printing market is newer than those in older industrial cities. Vetting a shop's equipment and process discipline is especially worthwhile here — capabilities vary more than in mature markets.
  • Automotive lead times: Smyrna and Murfreesboro shops serving the Nissan supply chain can be tight on capacity during model changeover periods. Check availability for time-sensitive automotive jobs in advance.
  • Entertainment applications: Nashville's entertainment and hospitality industry creates demand for unusual applications — prop fabrication, themed elements, custom fixtures. Several shops have developed expertise in these areas.
  • Healthcare compliance: Nashville shops serving the healthcare market are generally familiar with quality documentation requirements — ask specifically about ISO certifications and material traceability before ordering for clinical applications.
